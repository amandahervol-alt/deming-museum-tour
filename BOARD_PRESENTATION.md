# 🏛️ Deming Luna Mimbres Museum Tour & AI Docent
## Board of Directors Presentation Guide & Plain-Language Breakdown

> **Purpose**: A plain-English, conversational cheat sheet designed for speaking with museum board members, community leaders, and volunteer docents without using confusing tech jargon.

---

## 🎙️ The 60-Second Opening Pitch (Say This Out Loud)

> *"Imagine a family walks into our historic 1916 Armory. Instead of having to download a bulky app or wait for a scheduled tour guide, they just open their phone camera, point it at a small wooden placard on the wall, and within two seconds, they're listening to a warm audio tour of that exact room—or reading the story right on their screen.*
>
> *Even better: if a curious child or visitor wants to ask, 'Why is there a hole in that Mimbres pottery bowl?' or 'Where did Pancho Villa raid?', they tap a little button on their screen, and our virtual museum docent answers their question instantly—using only the verified history approved by our historical society.*
>
> *And best of all for our budget: **it costs the museum zero dollars to run, requires zero maintenance, and works on every smartphone.***"

---

## 🌟 The 4 Key Things the Board Cares About

### 1. 💰 What Does It Cost Us? ($0.00)
* **The Plain Answer**: It is 100% free to host and operate forever.
* **Why**: It runs on free civic web hosting (GitHub Pages). There are no monthly software subscriptions, no expensive tablet devices for the museum to buy or recharge, and no maintenance contracts.

---

### 2. 📱 How Easy Is It for Visitors? (Zero Downloads, Zero Sign-Ups)
* **The Plain Answer**: Visitors do **not** have to go to the App Store, download an app, create an account, or give their email address.
* **How it Works**: 
  1. Visitor walks into any of our 15 exhibit rooms (e.g., Mimbres Pottery, Railroad Room, Military Memorial).
  2. They open their standard phone camera and scan the room's QR code.
  3. The tour opens instantly in their browser with audio and clear text.

---

### 3. 🧱 What If the Cell Signal is Weak Inside Our Thick 1916 Brick Walls?
* **The Plain Answer**: We built it specifically with our historic building in mind.
* **How it Works**:
  * The audio files are tiny and load in a split second.
  * **Every single room has a full written transcript on screen**. If a visitor's phone signal is slow, or if they don't have headphones and don't want to disturb others, they can read the story comfortably.

---

### 4. 🤖 What is the "AI Docent" and Will It Make Things Up?
* **The Plain Answer**: It’s like having a friendly volunteer guide in their pocket who knows every detail of our exhibits.
* **Strict Fact-Checking**:
  * It is **grounded exclusively in our museum's approved historical facts** (Mimbres pottery, the 1881 transcontinental railroad junction, Bataan Death March veterans, Rockhound State Park, the Columbus raid).
  * If someone asks about local restaurants or Rockhound Park, it gives helpful local recommendations.
  * It works even when offline with built-in answers to the most common visitor questions.
