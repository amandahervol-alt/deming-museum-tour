In a town built largely of wood and dry adobe during the railroad era, fire protection was a matter of life and death. This gallery showcases early hand-drawn hose carts, brass fire extinguishers, leather helmets, and the original alarm bells that alerted Deming's volunteer firefighters to emergencies across town.
