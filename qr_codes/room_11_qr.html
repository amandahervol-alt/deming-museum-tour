📖 Audio Speech Narration Script 1:25

Upon this location's opening, the museum started to receive so many donations that it ran out of room and discussions were held to add additions. One such discussion was to recreate a store front exhibit of Deming. What you see first are horse drawn carriages. A hearse donated by Baca Funeral Home last used in the 90s; buggies most likely used for going to church and courting your special someone; a sleigh even though Deming only receives an average of nine inches of total precipitation per year; and various wagons used by families, farmers, and ranchers. To the right of the hearse is Deming's first traffic light and an old gas pump. Great additions! The recreated shops illustrate what items were available and applicable to the store. The Mahoney Hardware has the washing machine that is the reason this museum is what you see now. Dental office, beauty salon, dress shop, post office, the livery stable, funeral parlor with an old casket and clothing for burial purposes, barber shop and so much more. Several store fronts have original signs. Look closely at each store front to absorb the era of the time. The Deming Headlight is of particular interest as it is considered the oldest continuously published newspaper in the state even though it is twice a week now. A look in the window one will see an old typewriter and an over-engineered pencil sharpener We have their old, old, printing press. A big and very heavy piece of equipment.
