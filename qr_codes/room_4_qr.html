Welcome to the Military Room. Along the left wall are photographs of Luna County residents who served in the various branches of our Armed Forces, and the cabinets around you hold insignia, uniforms, mementos, and challenge coins donated and loaned by local families. Look for the faded black-and-white photo of the 200th and 515th Coast Artillery—the New Mexico servicemen who would later take part in the Bataan Death March. A full exhibit on the march is located near the museum's entrance. In the World War II section, you'll find uniforms, insignia, and mementos donated by veterans and their families, along with scale models of ships and planes. At the center of the room, a plexiglass case holds a Norden Mark XV Bombsight—the same model tested in B-17 bombers that trained right here at the Deming Army Airfield, where nearly 8,000 bombardiers were trained by the end of the war. Next, explore displays devoted to Fort Cummings, the first walled fort in New Mexico Territory (1863–1873) and home to the 9th and 10th Cavalry 'Buffalo Soldiers'. Learn about Camp Cody, Deming's massive WWI training camp named after Buffalo Bill Cody, which later became a tuberculosis sanitarium. Finally, view artifacts from the March 9, 1916 Pancho Villa Raid on Columbus, NM, including the historic machine gun that defended the town and General John J. Pershing's Punitive Expedition gear.
